# Judge demo script

## Setup

Start MongoDB access, Flask, Ollama with `qwen3:1.7b`, and Vite. Open **Demo Mode**. Point out that every scenario is synthetic and allowlisted.

## 1. Rahul — healthy progress

1. Launch **Healthy Customer — Rahul**.
2. On Dashboard, show the health score and positive proactive moment.
3. Open Financial Health and explain GROWTH, its factor evidence and the longer-term K-Means profile.
4. Open Recommendations and show a positive financial action plus “Why this?”.

## 2. Ramesh — support before sales

1. Return to Demo Mode and launch **Financial Stress — Ramesh**.
2. Show HIGH stress, negative/pressured surplus and the proactive Saathi alert.
3. Expand **Why?** to show expense burden, savings, EMI burden and monthly surplus.
4. Open Recommendations and show **Why NOT This?** for new borrowing.
5. Show the safer alternative and explain that wellbeing takes priority over sales.
6. Ask Saathi: `mari financial situation kem che?`
7. Point out the Gujarati answer and verified values.

## 3. Aman — anomaly intervention

1. Launch **Fraud Alert — Aman**.
2. Review the clearly labeled synthetic alert: **₹52,000 · 3:15 AM · new merchant · different location**.
3. Expand why it was flagged. Stress that this is a signal, not proof of fraud.
4. Click **This was me** or **Report suspicious** and explain that the prototype updates alert status only.

## 4. Safe digital journey

1. Open Loan Journey and show that income, obligations, surplus, stress and profile are already known.
2. Simulate ₹2 lakh at 8% for one year and show current → EMI → projected surplus.
3. Open KYC with Saathi and point out that no real identity number or document is collected.
4. Finish on Privacy & Responsible AI: consent, fairness, explainability, local AI and regulatory-readiness caveats.

## Close
