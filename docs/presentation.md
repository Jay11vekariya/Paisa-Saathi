# Paisa Saathi — 5–7 minute presentation

## Slide 1 — The problem (20 seconds)

Banking is often product-first and generic. Bharat needs guidance that understands cash flow, stress and language before suggesting the next step.

## Slide 2 — The solution (25 seconds)

Paisa Saathi is a customer-first banking companion: verified data becomes understanding, personalized explanation and proactive action. Tagline: **Your Money, Your Needs, Your Saathi.**

## Slide 3 — Architecture (25 seconds)

Show the architecture diagram. Emphasize React, Flask/JWT, MongoDB, deterministic financial intelligence and local Ollama/Qwen. No external AI API receives customer context.

## Slide 4 — Personalization engine (25 seconds)

Transactions produce health, stress and K-Means behavioural segments. The recommendation engine uses all three and explains both suitable and unsuitable options.

## Slide 5 — Financial stress (25 seconds)

Show Ramesh. Explain the HIGH stress evidence: cash flow, savings trend, EMI burden and buffer. The system switches from selling to support.

## Slide 6 — Proactive intervention (25 seconds)

Open Saathi Alerts. Show evidence, why it matters and an empathetic action. Alerts act on signals rather than merely displaying a score.

## Slide 7 — Loan Impact Simulator (30 seconds)

Enter a proposed amount, rate and tenure. Compare current surplus and burden with the projected state. Call it financial suitability guidance—not approval.

## Slide 8 — Vernacular Saathi AI (30 seconds)

Ask a Gujarati question. Explain that financial engines calculate first; local Qwen only expresses verified context naturally. A quality gate catches weak-model corruption.

## Slide 9 — Fraud/anomaly support (25 seconds)

Launch Aman’s clearly labeled demo. Review ₹52,000, 3:15 AM, new merchant and different-location signals. “This was me” and “Report suspicious” are prototype status actions.

## Slide 10 — Conversational onboarding and KYC (25 seconds)

Show language, goals and banking need captured during onboarding. Show the KYC prototype warning: no real Aadhaar/PAN or documents are accepted or stored.

## Slide 11 — Responsible AI (30 seconds)

Demonstrate consent toggles, explainability, data minimization, fairness guardrails, no predatory nudging and customer control. State that production requires formal regulatory controls.

## Slide 12 — Demo scenarios and scale (20 seconds)

Four allowlisted synthetic scenarios make the story repeatable. Modular services can later connect to bank-grade consent, KYC, fraud operations and human review.

## Slide 13 — Social impact and close (20 seconds)

The value is not another dashboard: it is earlier understanding, safer borrowing and accessible guidance in the customer’s language. **Your Money, Your Needs, Your Saathi.**
